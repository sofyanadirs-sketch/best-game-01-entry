Ojek Rush is an arcade game about Bang Udin, the fastest ojek driver in Jakarta at dusk. Pick up passengers waving at the roadside — Mbak Sari rushing to a kondangan, Pak RT late for his meeting, Bu Tini off to Pasar Senen — and ride each one through the green destination gate before their patience runs out. Dodge cars, bajaj, angkot, trucks and potholes, grab Rp coins on the way, and mind the meter: deliver 5 passengers inside a 90-second shift to win, crash 3 times or run out of time and the shift is over. All art is drawn in code (Monas on the skyline, warung stalls, billboards for Sate Ayam 10RB and Kopi Hitam), with synthesised WebAudio bleeps and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Steer with ← → or A / D, hold ↑ for gas, ↓ to brake, Space to start or restart, P to pause, or click the left / right half of the road to change lane.
